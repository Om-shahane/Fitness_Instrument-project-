About GymGear Guide

Plain-language education about gym equipment, so you can understand it before you use or buy it.

What we do

GymGear Guide helps people understand gym equipment. For each instrument we explain what it is for, which muscles it works, how a beginner can use it, what to watch for on safety and what it typically costs in India.

You can compare equipment types side by side and read short articles on choosing, using and maintaining gear. The goal is simple: help you make informed decisions through educational content.

How we handle prices

Prices are indicative ranges in Indian rupees, shown to help you plan. They are not live prices, and we do not publish seller listings, ratings or discounts. Always verify with current manufacturers or sellers.
