Why prices vary

The same type of equipment can cost very different amounts. These are the main reasons.

Brand

Established brands often charge for design, testing and a wider service network.

Material and build quality

Thicker steel, better welds, quality bearings and durable upholstery cost more to make.

Weight capacity

Equipment rated for heavier users or heavier loads needs a stronger frame.

Features

Motors, consoles, programs, incline and connectivity add to the price of cardio machines.

Warranty and service

Longer warranties and nearby service support are usually reflected in the price.

Intended use

Commercial-rated equipment is built for many users all day, so it costs more than home equipment.

Also ask sellers about delivery, installation and service plans, since these can change the total cost. Read more in Understanding Gym Equipment Price Differences.
